SF Archive Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-12

What the app does

SF Archive is a private tool used only by its owner. It uploads the owner's own backup and archive files to the owner's own Google Drive and reads them back when the owner restores them.

Data accessed

The app accesses only the Google Drive files it needs to store and restore the owner's archive. It does not access other users' data.

How data is used and shared

Data is used only to store and restore the owner's files. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Google user data is not transferred anywhere other than the owner's own computer and the owner's own Google Drive.

Storage and deletion

Files stay in the owner's Google Drive and on the owner's computer. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
